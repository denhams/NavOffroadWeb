Neither the NavOffroad Planner nor the NavOffroad iPad® and iPhone® app collect or retain any personal data. No location data are retained.

The Mapbox SDK used by the apps collects device location and select related data that is promptly de-identified, anonymized and aggregated to support and improve its products and services. Please refer to the Mapbox Privacy Policy. You can opt out of Mapbox telemetry at any time. Tap the Information (i) button in the bottom-right corner of the app, select Mapbox Telemetry, then tap Stop Participating.

The what3words APIs used by the iPad® and iPhone® app may collect non-personal metadata about a request that can include the IP address. Please see the section "(G) The what3words API" of the what3words privacy policy and the Woosmap APIs General Terms and Conditions.

The Geoapify API used by the iPad® and iPhone® app may collect non-personal metadata about a request that can include the IP address. Please see the Geoapify privacy policy.
